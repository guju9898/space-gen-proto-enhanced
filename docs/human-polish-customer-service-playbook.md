Items marked **OWNER REVIEW** require Frank (or designated owner) before promising an exception.

---

## 1. “I can do this myself with ChatGPT.”

**Approved response:**

You’re right that AI tools can generate images. Human Polish™ is different: you get a **done-for-you production team** working from a prescriptive brief — organized concepts, quality control, and a branded PDF package — without you prompting, sorting, or managing freelancers.

Many contractors use **Renderspace** to explore quickly themselves, then use **Human Polish** when the project needs a real team behind it.

Happy to help you pick the right path for this job.

---

## 2. “Why are there no revisions on AI Render Packs?”

**Approved response:**

AI Render Packs are concept-generation packages, not open-ended design retainers. Instead of unlimited revisions, you get the **First-Batch Brief-Match Guarantee**:

We deliver the first 5 / 10 / 15 concepts (depending on pack size) before finishing the rest. If that first batch materially fails to follow the **approved written brief**, tell us within **24 hours** and we’ll correct the direction once at no extra charge before producing the remaining concepts.

A new property, new design direction, or new client request after approval is **not** covered as a brief-match correction.

---

## 3. “The first batch is not what I imagined.”

**Clarify first:** Is this a brief-match failure, or a change of direction?

**If brief-match (within 24 hours):**  
Thanks — please point to the specific must-haves / avoids from the approved brief that weren’t followed. We’ll correct the direction once under the Brief-Match Guarantee, then complete the pack.

**If change of mind / new direction:**  
Understood. A new direction isn’t covered by the Brief-Match Guarantee. Options are to continue under the approved brief, purchase an additional pack, or discuss a custom arrangement.

**OWNER REVIEW:** Goodwill remakes outside the guarantee.

---

## 4. “My client changed their mind.”

**Approved response:**

That happens. Changes after brief approval (new must-haves, new style, new scope) are treated as a **new direction**, not a free correction. We can finish the purchased pack to the original brief, or quote a new pack / custom fee for the updated direction.

---

## 5. “Can I use one pack for several properties?”

**Approved response:**

A standard pack covers **one project at one property**. A second property may be approved only after a short call when both share substantially the same design objective and can be produced as one coherent cycle. Otherwise, separate properties need separate packs.

---

## 6. “Can you guarantee HOA approval?”

**Approved response:**

No. We can deliver an **HOA submission-ready presentation package** designed to **support** HOA and design-review submissions. Approval decisions belong to the HOA / design-review board. We don’t guarantee HOA approval.

---

## 7. “Are these permit plans?”

**Approved response:**

No. Human Polish packages are for **visualization, client presentation, and HOA/design-review support**. They do **not** include architectural, structural, civil, or engineering services; professional seals; permit drawings; construction documents; or code-compliance certification.

---

## 8. “I need this tomorrow.”

**Approved response:**

Rush is available on some AI Render Packs **only after manual capacity approval**:

- 25 concepts: guaranteed 24-hour delivery for $79 (after approval + file acceptance)  
- 50 concepts: guaranteed 48-hour delivery for $79  
- 100 concepts: contact us for custom rush terms  

Rush is never automatic. The delivery clock still starts only after we accept your files as complete and usable.

---

## 9. “My photos are not very good.”

**Approved response:**

We’ll be honest in file review. If photos aren’t usable, we’ll pause production, request replacements, and **won’t start the delivery clock** until files are accepted. If fulfillment remains impossible before production begins, we may cancel and refund rather than deliver unusable work.

Clear daytime photos of the work area (and a survey for Build-Ready) make the biggest difference.

---

## 10. “Why has my delivery clock not started?”

**Approved response:**

The clock does **not** start at payment alone. It starts only after payment **and** Renderspace confirms your files and instructions are complete and usable. You’ll get a **Files Accepted** email when that happens. If we’re waiting on replacements or clarification, the order stays active but the clock remains paused.

---

## 11. “Can I get a refund?”

**Policy anchors (do not expand casually):**

- **Unusable files before production:** may cancel and refund rather than deliver unusable work.  
- **Missed approved rush deadline (our control):** rush **fee** refunded; base package not refunded solely for missing rush if work remains deliverable.  

**OWNER REVIEW:** All other refund requests (change of mind after production start, dissatisfaction outside Brief-Match rules, partial refunds, chargebacks). Do not invent a general “30-day money-back” policy.

**Approved holding reply:**  
I’ve logged your refund request for request {requestId}. Frank / ownership will review against our file-usability and rush-fee policies and reply with a clear decision.

---

## 12. “I need more than 100 concepts.”

**Approved response:**

V1 checkout is **one pack per order** (max 100 concepts per pack). For larger volume, ongoing production, or a special arrangement, let’s schedule a short team call or email frank@renderspace.ai with your volume and timeline.

---

## 13. “Can you add another property?”

Same as #5. Do not silently expand scope. Route to call / review.

---

## 14. “Can I receive editable source files?”

**Approved response:**

Standard delivery is high-resolution **PNG** files plus an organized **PDF**. Editable source files are **not** included unless separately agreed.

**OWNER REVIEW:** Any source-file exception or fee.

---

## 15. “Can you do commercial work?”

**Approved response:**

Yes — commercial / large-property work is supported, but nonstandard or large scopes often go through **manual review or custom pricing** (Build-Ready Custom or a scoped call). Submit through intake or email frank@renderspace.ai with site details.

We still do not provide sealed engineering / permit / construction documents.

---

## 16. “What happens after 90 days?”

**Approved response:**

AI Render Packs must be used within **90 days of purchase**. That window is shown before checkout and in acknowledgments. After expiration, unused production capacity is not guaranteed.

**OWNER REVIEW:** Any extension requests.

---

## 17. “Can you brand the PDF for my company?”

**Approved response:**

Yes — when you request branding in intake and provide company name, logo, phone, website, and any brand notes, we can apply contractor/company branding on the organized PDF.

---

## 18. “What is the difference between Renderspace and Human Polish?”

**Approved response:**

**Renderspace** is rapid self-serve exploration and visual selling.  
**Human Polish** is your outsourced visualization department — our team produces the concepts or Build-Ready presentation package for you.

Use Renderspace to explore quickly. Use Human Polish when the project needs a real team behind it.

---

## 19. “What is the difference between AI Packs and Essentials 3D?”

**Approved response:**

**AI Render Packs** generate batches of conceptual design renders from your brief (25 / 50 / 100), with a first-batch brief-match check — not open-ended revisions.

**Essentials 3D (Build-Ready)** starts from an approved direction and produces a professional presentation package: Essentials 2D plans/materials plus **three guaranteed human-produced photorealistic views** (and up to two more supporting views when scope allows), with limited revision rounds. Build-Ready always requires human scope review before payment.

---

## 20. “Can you make construction drawings?”

**Approved response:**

No. We do not provide construction documents, shop drawings, stamped plans, or permit drawings. We produce visualization and presentation materials based on the survey, photos, approved concept, and information you provide.
